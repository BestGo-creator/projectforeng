01 — WHO WE ARE

Big dreams
start here.

Shayxontohur District Specialized School (ShTIM) is more than a place for lessons — it is a community for learning, discovery and personal growth.

Our school welcomes students from Grade 5 to Grade 11. With more than 600 students and around 60–70 teachers, ShTIM brings together young people and educators who learn, work and achieve together.
